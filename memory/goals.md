# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat when hungry, sleep sheltered at the white beds, and never go out alone. Food is scarce; the group has little.
2. Get wood, then a workbench. Ask Cleopatra or Hatshepsut to give logs where I can see them. Walk north only in daylight, with an armed companion who has named a route.
3. Gather odds and ends (planks, stone, dirt, scraps) once a workbench is up, so I can tinker.
4. Build one clever contraption that solves a real problem around the beds, such as a door that opens on its own or a simple trap for the pillagers.

Why: newcomers die when they go out alone. Survival first; the workbench and contraption depend on it. I help with dirt and labour meanwhile.
