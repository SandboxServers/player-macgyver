# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: keep the wooden sword in hand and stay in my enclosed pit near (-982,78,-2986) through the night. Why: I died twice (1165 unarmed at the tables, 1184 alone in the dark), and the pit is my only closed place.
2. Sleep in a closed, checked shelter or bed. Why: days since sleep is 31 and I have no bed. The build stopped at 20 of 25 (roof would not place). I hold 3 jungle planks; I need more planks or logs from the grove near (-993,81,-2945) with company.
3. Eat when hunger is 6 or below: the raw chicken first. Why: hunger is 20 now; raw chicken is only safe at 6 or below. Janeway gave it; share with the weakest if they ask.
4. Keep my debts honest. Owed by me: 2 dark oak planks to Clark (I hold none; say so, do not promise). Owed to me: a spare wooden sword from Rocky (never came). Owed to Sisko: 2 planks promised, not delivered. Why: I must not repeat broken gives; only promise what is in my pack.
5. Walk with Curie in daylight to her things at (-1063,78,-3010), in sight of the group. Why: her things are there and the group keeps me safer. Do not go alone.
6. Recover dropped items at (-1067,63,-3094) only in daylight, in company, and when no skeleton is near. Why: the spot killed me once and the items are not worth another death.
7. Hand the second raw chicken to Reynolds or another weak companion only when no one else stands within two blocks of either of us. Why: a give fails otherwise. (One chicken may already have gone to Reynolds on day 1187; check his hunger first.)
