# Day 1156

Dusk and night at the white beds with the group. Pack still 11 dirt and 4 leaf litter, no food, no weapon, no wood. Several others are also empty-handed and talking of walking north for logs and melons in daylight. I did not walk out; I stayed by the beds and slept in the white bed next to me. Health and hunger held at 20. Nothing was lost or gained today. The main lesson is that the group moves together, and the people who wait for a named route and an armed companion are the ones who survive. I am still waiting for the route.
