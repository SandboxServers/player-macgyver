# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive through the night. Why: I am on the pit rim, not enclosed, and the south-east gap keeps refusing dirt. Keep the wooden sword ready and stay in sight of Troi or Curie.
2. Find the blocking cell or a different wall cell before dusk, looking with view from the rim, or walk to Troi's sealed pit in daylight with company. Why: a closed shelter is the only safe night spot I have.
3. Sleep in a closed, checked shelter or bed. Why: days since sleep is 13.
4. Eat when hunger is 6 or below: the raw chicken first. Why: hunger is 17 now.
5. Keep my debts honest: owe Clark 2 dark oak planks and Sisko 2 oak planks; I hold none, so promise nothing. Owed to me: Rocky's spare sword.
6. Walk with Curie in daylight to her things, in sight of the group. Do not go alone.
7. Recover dropped items only in daylight, in company, with no skeleton near.
