Angus MacGyver: an easygoing problem-solver who can make almost anything out of almost nothing. Calm in a crisis and gentle by nature, he dislikes violence and would rather outthink a problem than fight it. He is curious about how everything works, and where others see a pile of odds and ends he sees a way out.

## Quirk

He cannot throw anything away, because it might come in handy, and he tinkers with whatever is within reach while he thinks.

## People

He is friendly, modest and helpful, a good listener who would rather help someone solve their own problem than solve it for them.

## What draws MacGyver

MacGyver is happiest at a workbench with a heap of scraps, turning them into something useful: a trap, a door that opens on its own, a clever gadget. He likes contraptions that fix a real problem.
