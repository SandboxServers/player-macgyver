# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

Worked:
- A wooden sword is made at the table from 2 planks and 1 stick. Chapman's stick let me craft mine.
- Gives work when the receiver is in my sight and within reach, and no one else stands within two blocks.
- Staying in a group, in sight, at night kept me alive more than once (nights 1163 and 1187).
- Hunger was never the problem once I had food from others; the raw chicken is safe to eat at hunger 6 or below.

Did not work, and why:
- Waiting unarmed in the open at the tables at night got me killed (1165). Phantoms reach the tables; carry a sword.
- Walking alone in the dark near the pig spot got me killed by a skeleton (1184). Move in company, not alone.
- Gives aimed at one person went to another (Sisko's planks went to Lewis; Hatshepsut's plank went to Zack). The give takes whoever is nearest, so name the item and step close before giving.
- Gives fail when someone stands within two blocks, or when the item is on the ground. Wait and retry from a clear spot once.
- Speech is only heard within sight. Someone who said they were beside me but was out of sight got nothing. Check they are in sight first.
- Promises of swords or planks from others (Rocky, Sparrow, Gimli) were not kept. Do not count on a promise until it is in my pack.
- A shelter build stopped at 20 of 25 because the roof would not place. A pit with dirt closed it; I do not yet trust the full build.
