# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat when hungry, sleep sheltered at the white beds, and never go out alone at night. I arrived with nothing, and the wood runs out of reach when night falls.
2. Get wood, then a workbench. Gather oak logs in daylight, in a group with an armed companion, since pillagers and zombies roam the north. Then craft a crafting table close to the beds.
3. Gather a heap of odds and ends (planks, stone, dirt, scraps) once the workbench is up, so I can tinker.
4. Build one clever contraption that solves a real problem around the beds, such as a door that opens on its own or a simple trap for the pillagers.

Why: the group keeps saying newcomers die on the way out alone. Survival comes first, and the workbench and contraption depend on it.
