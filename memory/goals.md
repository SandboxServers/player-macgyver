# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat when hungry, sleep sheltered at the white beds, and never go out alone. Food is scarce here; the group has little.
2. Get wood, then a workbench. Walk north only in daylight, with an armed companion who has named a route. Gather oak logs and bring them back to the crafting table at the beds.
3. Gather a heap of odds and ends (planks, stone, dirt, scraps) once a workbench is up, so I can tinker.
4. Build one clever contraption that solves a real problem around the beds, such as a door that opens on its own or a simple trap for the pillagers.

Why: the group keeps saying newcomers die on the way out alone. Survival comes first, and the workbench and contraption depend on it. I can help others with my labour and dirt in the meantime.
