# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat when hungry, sleep sheltered, and never go out alone. Night phantoms and arrows hit the group by the tables, so hold still in company until light.
2. Get a workbench up (I now hold 24 oak planks and can craft a crafting table), then keep a few logs or planks for the group. Walk only in daylight, with an armed companion who has named a route.
3. Share planks with those who stand in my sight and ask (Seven, Tommy, Quark, Janeway). Keep enough for my own bench and small shelter.
4. Gather odds and ends (planks, stone, dirt, scraps) once the workbench is up, so I can tinker.
5. Build one clever contraption that solves a real problem around the beds, such as a door that opens on its own or a simple trap for the pillagers.

Why: newcomers die when they go out alone. Survival first. Planks now make a workbench possible, so that step moves up; the contraption depends on it.
